# Baru Budget v0.6 — Credit Cards and Invoices

## Status

IN_PROGRESS — architectural analysis delivered (`docs/ARCHITECTURE_ANALYSIS_V0.6.md`); implementation awaits approval.

## Objective

Record credit card purchases and follow invoices: cards with limit, closing day and due day; a purchase attached to the card and to the correct invoice; invoices with total, due date and status; invoice payment recorded as a transfer, so a card expense is never counted twice.

## Scope

- Database: `credit_cards` table; immutable `invoice_due_date_for` function; `credit_card_id` and `invoice_due_date` columns on `transactions`; `account_id` becomes optional; new shape constraint covering card purchases and invoice payments; card ownership validation; indexes; RLS and policies; `credit_card_invoices` view.
- Explicit invoice competence rule covering closing, due date, month turn, year turn and non-existent days.
- Cards page with limit, used and available amounts, next invoice; create, edit, activate/deactivate, delete.
- Card detail with invoices by due date, their transactions, total, status and payment action.
- Transaction form: expenses paid with an account or with a card, invoice preview, transfers to a card invoice; card filter on the transactions page.
- Dashboard card with unpaid invoices due in the period.
- pgTAP and Vitest tests sharing the same case table for the competence rule.
- Migrations applied to the hosted project before the frontend deploy.

## Known Limitations

- Installments arrive in v0.7; a card purchase is a single entry in a single invoice.
- Refunds on a card purchase are not supported yet.
- Revolving credit and invoice interest are not modelled.

## Out of Scope

Installments, fixed expenses and recurrences, expense splitting on card purchases, card refunds, third-party card invoices, shared limits, invoice import, interest.

## Completion Criteria

- `npm run db:reset` applies all migrations; `npm run db:types` produces no diff.
- `npm run test:db`, `npm test`, `npm run lint` and `npm run build` pass.
- Every table in `public` has RLS enabled; every view has `security_invoker = true`.
- pgTAP covers every row of the competence table, including month and year turns and non-existent days.
- A card purchase counts as an expense of the period and never touches account balances; an invoice payment debits the account and never counts as an expense.
- Migrations applied to the hosted project; `credit_cards` and `credit_card_invoices` return an empty list to the publishable key.
- In the application: create a card, record purchases before and after the closing day, see them in the right invoices, register the payment and confirm the account balance drops without changing the expenses of the period.
