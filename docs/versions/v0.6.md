## Objective

Record credit card purchases and follow invoices: cards with limit, closing day and due day; a purchase attached to the card and to the correct invoice; invoices with total, due date and status; invoice payment recorded as a transfer, so a card expense is never counted twice. ## Technical Behavior

- **Invoice competence.** `public.invoice_due_date_for(purchase_date, closing_day, due_day)` is immutable and is the authority. The closing day is clamped to the last day of the month; a purchase on or before the closing day belongs to the invoice closing that month and a later one to the next; the invoice is due in the closing month when the due day is after the closing day, otherwise in the following month, clamped the same way. The TypeScript mirror in `invoice.ts` only powers the form preview; both are covered by the same case table.
- **The invoice is identified by its due date**, stored on the purchase by the validation trigger when the client does not send one. Changing the closing day of a card later does not move invoices already recorded.
- **No double counting.** A card purchase is an EXPENSE with no account: it counts in the expenses of the period and never touches `account_balances`. Paying the invoice is a TRANSFER from an account to the card: it debits the account and never counts as an expense. The rule is enforced by the shape constraint, not by a report adjustment.
- **Exactly one origin per transaction**, covering four cases: income or expense in an account, transfer between two own accounts, card purchase, invoice payment. `account_id` became optional to allow the card purchase case.
- Card purchases accept PAID and CANCELLED only; invoices count PAID purchases. Partial payments are allowed and the invoice reports total, paid and remaining.
- Invoice status is derived in the client: PAID when the paid amount reaches the total, PARTIAL when something was paid, CLOSED when the closing date has passed, OPEN otherwise. Card usage is the sum of what is still owed across unpaid invoices; the available amount is shown only when the card has a limit.
- A card belongs to a user, like accounts and categories: read with `can_view`, write with `can_manage`. A card is not an account and never enters the money balance. Deleting a card with transactions is refused (`23503`); the interface offers deactivation.
- The card detail page loads the transactions of the selected invoice on demand, so opening a card does not fetch its whole history.

## Security Changes

- Card ownership is validated in the trigger: a purchase or payment referencing a card of another user fails with `23503`.
- `credit_card_invoices` runs with the caller privileges, so invoice totals never expose transactions the user cannot read.
- The shape constraint refuses a transaction with both an account and a card, a card purchase without a category, an income on a card, and an invoice payment without a source account or without an invoice.
- Verified through PostgREST on the local stack: purchases land on the expected invoices, only the payment debits the account, and `anon` sees neither cards nor invoices.

## Known Limitations

- Installments arrive in v0.7; a card purchase is a single entry in a single invoice.
- Refunds on a card purchase are not supported: an income cannot be attached to a card.
- Revolving credit and invoice interest are not modelled.
- The limit is informative and never blocks a purchase.

## Out of Scope

Installments, fixed expenses and recurrences, expense splitting on card purchases, card refunds, third-party card invoices, shared limits, invoice import, interest.
