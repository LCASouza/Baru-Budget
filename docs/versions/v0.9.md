# Baru Budget v0.9 — Allocations and Settlements

## Status

IN_PROGRESS — architectural analysis delivered (`docs/ARCHITECTURE_ANALYSIS_V0.9.md`); implementation awaits approval.

## Objective

Separate who paid from who is responsible and settle accounts between people: split an expense between users, compute what each one owes, record the payment between people and show receivables, payables and the net balance.

## Scope

- Database: `transaction_allocations` with the sum invariant enforced by deferred constraint triggers, `settlement_direction` enum, `counterparty_user_id` on `transactions`, SETTLEMENT unblocked, settlement shape, `account_balances` updated, visibility for the counterparty, `people_balances` view.
- `set_transaction_allocations` replacing the split of a transaction atomically.
- Split section in the transaction form with equal split and sum validation.
- Settlements page with receivables, payables, net balance, per-person balances and items, and settlement recording.
- Receivables and payables cards back on the dashboard.
- Split mark in the transactions list.
- pgTAP and Vitest tests.
- Migrations applied to the hosted project before the frontend deploy.

## Known Limitations

- A settlement is recorded against a person, not against specific items.
- The database accepts an allocation to any profile; the interface only offers household members and grant counterparts.
- Splitting is available on expenses only.

## Out of Scope

Stored percentages, income splitting, settling specific items, reminders and charging, loans and financings.
