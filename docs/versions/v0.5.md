## Technical Behavior

- The selected month reuses the transactions already loaded by `TransactionsStore` for the active context, without the page filters, so moving between Dashboard and Movimentações does not repeat the query. Only the six-month series has its own query.
- `monthly_transaction_totals` groups by owner, household, month and kind, excluding CANCELLED and keeping only INCOME and EXPENSE. `security_invoker` applies the row level security of `transactions` before aggregation: a VIEW grantee sums the owner's transactions, a household member sums only the transactions tagged with the household, and an unrelated user sums nothing.
- Personal and shared contexts query the view by `owner_user_id` and sum the rows of every `household_id` on the client, because a transaction of the user tagged with a household is still the user's expense. A household context queries by `household_id` and sums across members.
- Cards: Receitas, Despesas, Saldo do período and Pendentes in every context (competence: PAID and PENDING, no transfers, no cancelled). Saldo monetário and Benefícios only outside a household context (cash: opening balance plus PAID movements), because a household has no accounts of its own.
- The evolution chart covers six months ending at the selected month; months without data are drawn as zero to keep the axis continuous. The axis step is chosen as 1, 2 or 5 times a power of ten for about four ticks, so hundreds and hundreds of thousands both stay readable.
- Expenses by category keep the eight largest and collapse the rest into "Outras"; transactions whose category is not visible are grouped as "Sem categoria". In a household context the same chart shows expenses by person, using the member names resolved by the context.
- Pending items list the PENDING transactions of the month ordered with the overdue ones first, then by due date, falling back to the transaction date when there is no due date.
- The dashboard reloads when the month or the context changes because the resource parameters derive from both.

## Security Changes

- The aggregated view runs with the caller privileges, so totals never expose data the user cannot read; `rls_enabled.test.sql` already requires `security_invoker` on every view in `public`.
- Cash cards are hidden in a household context, keeping personal balances out of shared views.
- Verified through PostgREST on the local stack: the owner sees own totals split by household, a household member sees only household rows of both members, a VIEW grantee sees the owner totals, `anon` sees nothing.

## Known Limitations

- The dashboard has not been checked in production yet; the behaviour is verified by tests and on the local stack only.
- Receivables, payables and settlements return in v0.9.
- Cards for credit cards, installments, fixed expenses, loans and financings arrive with their versions.
- The evolution chart covers a fixed window of six months.
- A month with more than 1000 transactions is truncated by the PostgREST row limit (DEBT-002), which also affects the dashboard cards and lists of that month; the six-month chart is unaffected because it reads aggregated rows.

## Out of Scope

Receivables and payables between people, credit cards and invoices, installments, fixed expenses, loans, financings, balance evolution chart, income by source chart, custom date ranges, export.
