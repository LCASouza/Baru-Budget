# Baru Budget v0.5 — Dashboard

## Status

IN_PROGRESS — architectural analysis delivered (`docs/ARCHITECTURE_ANALYSIS_V0.5.md`); implementation awaits approval.

## Objective

Replace the mock dashboard with real data that follows the selected period and financial context: summary cards, six-month income and expense evolution, expenses by category, expenses by person in households, pending transactions of the period and recent transactions.

## Scope

- Database: `monthly_transaction_totals` view (`security_invoker`) with monthly income and expense totals by owner and household.
- Summary cards by context: Receitas, Despesas, Saldo do período, Pendentes; Saldo monetário and Benefícios in the personal and shared contexts.
- Six-month income × expense chart ending at the selected month, with an adaptive scale.
- Expenses by category in the period; in a household, also expenses by person.
- Pending transactions of the period (overdue first) and recent transactions with a link to the full page.
- Empty and loading states; the dashboard reacts to the period and to the context.
- Removal of the mock data and of the settlements component (returns in v0.9 with real data).
- pgTAP tests for the view and Vitest tests for the pure calculations, chart scale and store.
- Migration applied to the hosted project before the frontend deploy.

## Known Limitations

- Receivables, payables and settlements return in v0.9.
- Cards for credit cards, installments, fixed expenses, loans and financings arrive with their versions.
- The evolution chart covers a fixed window of six months.

## Out of Scope

Receivables and payables between people, credit cards and invoices, installments, fixed expenses, loans, financings, balance evolution chart, income by source chart, custom date ranges, export.
