# Baru Budget v0.8 — Recurrences

## Status

IN_PROGRESS — architectural analysis delivered (`docs/ARCHITECTURE_ANALYSIS_V0.8.md`); implementation awaits approval.

## Objective

Register what repeats every month and turn it into real transactions: fixed expenses (rent, power, internet, subscriptions) and recurring incomes (salary, meal vouchers, benefits), with monthly instances that can be edited without changing the template.

## Scope

- Database: `recurrence_frequency` enum, `fixed_expenses` and `recurring_incomes` tables with RLS and reference validation, `fixed_expense_id`, `recurring_income_id` and `recurrence_month` columns on `transactions`, shape constraint, partial unique indexes and the idempotent `generate_recurrences` function.
- Explicit generation per month, with no read side effects and no scheduler.
- Fixed expenses page with two tabs, the status of each template in the selected month and a "generate" action.
- Monthly instances editable as ordinary transactions.
- Recurrence mark in the transactions list.
- pgTAP and Vitest tests.
- Migrations applied to the hosted project before the frontend deploy.

## Known Limitations

- Generation is triggered by the user for a chosen month; there is no scheduler.
- Only monthly and yearly frequencies are supported.
- Future months are only projected by generating their transactions.

## Out of Scope

Scheduled generation, weekly and custom frequencies, programmed amount adjustments, projections without transactions, splitting instances between people, loans and financings, due date notifications.
