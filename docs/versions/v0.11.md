# Baru Budget v0.11 — Financings

## Status

IN_PROGRESS — architectural analysis delivered on 2026-09-07 (`docs/ARCHITECTURE_ANALYSIS_V0.11.md`). Implementation starts after its approval.

## Objective

Record financings of assets and follow what is left to pay: asset value, down payment, financed amount, amortization system, instalments generated month by month and outstanding balance, with the asset value never becoming a transaction so acquisition and instalments can never be counted twice.

## Scope

- Database: `financing_system` enum, `financings` table with a generated `financed_amount`, financing columns on `transactions`, shape constraint, partial unique indexes, `financing_monthly_rate`, `financing_installment_amounts` and `generate_financing_schedule`.
- Two explicit amortization systems with documented, tested formulas: Price and SAC.
- The non-duplication rule of MASTER_PROMPT section 35, enforced by shape rather than by report adjustment.
- Financings page, financing detail with the amortization schedule, financing form with a live preview.
- Financings card on the dashboard; financing mark in the transactions list.
- Amortization math promoted to `shared/finance`, shared by loans and financings.
- pgTAP and Vitest math tests, including the non-duplication tests.
- Migrations applied to the hosted project before the frontend deploy.

## Technical Behavior

Pending implementation. The analysis defines: the asset value is not a transaction, only the down payment and the instalments are; a financing produces no income because the money goes from the bank straight to the seller; `financed_amount` is a generated column; the yearly rate conversion is always effective; the cents difference lands on the last instalment; generation is idempotent and runs with invoker rights.
