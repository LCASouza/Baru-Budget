# Baru Budget v0.7 — Installments

## Status

IN_PROGRESS — architectural analysis delivered (`docs/ARCHITECTURE_ANALYSIS_V0.7.md`); implementation awaits approval.

## Objective

Record installment purchases and follow the future commitment: a purchase in N instalments generates N transactions, each in its own competence (card invoice or account month), with current instalment, remaining ones, next instalment and total committed.

## Scope

- Database: `installment_group_id`, `installment_number` and `installment_count` columns on `transactions`; installment shape constraint; index; `split_installment_amounts` and `create_installment_purchase` functions; `installment_purchases` view.
- Amount split in cents with the remainder on the first instalment.
- Card instalments in consecutive invoices; account instalments as monthly pending entries.
- Installments page with total and monthly commitment, purchases with progress, next instalment and remaining amount, instalments loaded on demand, filters by origin and status.
- Transaction form with an instalment count field and a preview.
- "4/12" shown in the transactions list and in the invoice detail.
- Dashboard card with the commitment after the period.
- Deleting a whole installment purchase.
- pgTAP and Vitest tests sharing the split and competence case tables.
- Migrations applied to the hosted project before the frontend deploy.

## Known Limitations

- An installment purchase can only be created as a whole; editing one instalment never changes the others and there is no bulk edit.
- Interest inside an installment purchase, early settlement and renegotiation are not modelled.
- Splitting instalments between people arrives in v0.9.

## Out of Scope

Bulk editing of an installment purchase, early settlement with discount, interest, renegotiation, splitting between people, fixed expenses and recurrences, income instalments.
