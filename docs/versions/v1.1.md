# Baru Budget v1.1 — Correção Monetária

## Status

IN_PROGRESS — architectural analysis approved on 2026-09-08 (`docs/ARCHITECTURE_ANALYSIS_V1.1.md`), with its five blocking decisions resolved the same day.

## Objective

Make an indexed debt readable without lying about it. The outstanding balance and the instalment on screen match what the lender charges, and when they cannot, the system says why instead of showing a projection with the face of a fact.

## Scope

- Observed statements: what the lender reported for a competence, recorded instead of guessed.
- Instalment charges: insurance and operational fee exist in the model and compose the amount of the generated transaction.
- Schedule as a function by parts, reanchored on the most recent statement.
- Outstanding balance with provenance: observed when there is an observation, projected otherwise, and the screen says which.
- Loans, because the problem is identical.
- Excel schema version 2, with a converter from version 1.
- Contracted principal separated from the amount actually released, which is what financed tax makes different.

Out of scope: everything in MASTER_PROMPT section 65; forecasting an index; fetching TR, IPCA or IGPM from an external service; renegotiation, portability, extraordinary amortization and early settlement.

## Technical Behavior

- **One table, not two.** The analysis named `financing_statements` and described the loan table as analogous. It became a single `debt_statements` carrying either `loan_id` or `financing_id`, exactly one of them, the way `transactions` already models a debt link. Two tables would have duplicated the policies, the tests and the workbook sheet without describing anything the single table cannot.
- **Competence is a month.** The competence is stored as the first day of the month and constrained to it, so two statements of the same month collide instead of both counting.
- **A statement with no debt is refused by row level security**, not by the shape constraint: with no debt there is no owner, so the policy denies before the constraint is reached. The constraint holds for the case that does resolve an owner, where a statement claiming two debts at once is refused.
- **Deleting the debt deletes its statements.** They describe one contract and mean nothing without it, which is why the link cascades rather than being cleared like a transaction's.
- **The schedule length comes from the last statement, not from `installment_count`.** A lender reporting a different number of remaining instalments changes how many there are; the contract column keeps recording what was signed.
- **A charge of an observed month comes from the statement, and any other month from the contract.** Insurance is usually recalculated over the balance and the borrower's age, so the observed value wins where it exists.
- **The competence is a month, and the dialog drops the day.** The picker takes a date because that is the control the application already has, and only the month reaches the database.
- **Recording a statement for a month that already has one replaces it**, through an upsert on the debt and the competence, so correcting a typo does not need a delete first.
- **The amount written on the transaction is instalment plus insurance plus fee**, which is what leaves the account. The split stays available through the schedule and the statement, so the ledger is right without hiding how much of the payment is interest.
- **Measured against the real contract:** with the September 2026 statement recorded, the schedule projects R$ 1.129,29 against the R$ 1.125,09 charged, and the transaction lands R$ 4,20 from the R$ 1.181,68 actually billed. Before the statement the same contract projected R$ 1.082,63, R$ 99,05 short.

## Security Changes

- `debt_statements` carries no `owner_user_id`. The parent debt decides, resolved by `debt_owner`, which mirrors how `transaction_allocations` reaches the owner of its transaction.
- The table joins the audit columns and both triggers, so the transversal assertion that every audited table has them keeps holding.
- `debt_owner` pins `search_path`, is revoked from `public` and `anon`, and is granted to `authenticated` only.
- Nothing new is sensitive: the balance and the instalment of a debt are already visible to whoever sees the debt.

## Tests

- pgTAP: `debt_statements.test.sql` and `schedule_by_parts.test.sql` join the suite. Result so far: 37 files, 1034 assertions, all passing.
- Vitest: `buildScheduleByParts` covered in `amortization.spec.ts` against the same fixtures the pgTAP file asserts, so the two sides cannot drift; `loan.model.spec.ts` covers the provenance of the balance and the charge fallback; `loans.store.spec.ts` covers recording a statement. Result so far: 65 files, 413 tests, all passing.
- The whole suite passing unchanged before the new tests is the proof that a debt without statements and without charges generates exactly what it generated in v1.0.
- The transversal files needed no change and already cover the new table, because they scan `pg_class` instead of listing tables: row level security enabled, at least one policy, no policy open to `anon` or `public`, and both audit triggers present.
