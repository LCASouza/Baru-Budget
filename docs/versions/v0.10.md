# Baru Budget v0.10 — Loans

## Status

IN_PROGRESS — architectural analysis delivered (`docs/ARCHITECTURE_ANALYSIS_V0.10.md`); implementation awaits approval.

## Objective

Record loans taken and follow what is left to pay: principal, rate, an explicit interest model, instalments generated month by month, outstanding principal and remaining total, with the disbursement increasing cash without inflating the income of the month.

## Scope

- Database: `loan_interest_model` and `interest_period` enums, `loans` table, `loan_id` and `loan_installment_number` on `transactions`, shape constraint, partial unique indexes, `generate_loan_schedule`, and `monthly_transaction_totals` excluding the disbursement from income.
- Two explicit interest models with documented, tested formulas and rate conversions.
- Loans page with the list, the amortization schedule and the form.
- Loans card on the dashboard; loan mark in the transactions list.
- pgTAP and Vitest math tests.
- Migrations applied to the hosted project before the frontend deploy.

## Known Limitations

- Only loans taken are covered; lending money with interest is not modelled.
- Renegotiation, early amortization, grace periods, insurance and fees are not modelled.
- Deleting a loan keeps its transactions and the disbursement goes back to counting as income.

## Out of Scope

Loans granted with interest, renegotiation, portability, early amortization, grace periods, insurance and fees, compound interest without instalments, financings.
